SATHYABAMA INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY - OFFICIAL CAMPUS KNOWLEDGE BASE

LOCATION:
- Address: Jeppiaar Nagar, Rajiv Gandhi Salai, Old Mamallapuram Road (OMR), Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600119.

CAMPUS TIMINGS & RULES:
- Standard Working Hours: 9:00 AM to 3:15 PM (Monday to Friday).
- Grace Period: Day scholars are permitted entry up to 9:30 AM. Nobody is permitted inside the campus after 9:30 AM.
- ID Cards: Wearing the physical ID card with the official lanyard is mandatory at all times inside the campus.
- Dress Code: Formal attire is strictly enforced for all students. Jeans and T-shirts are not permitted during class hours.
- Mobile Phones: Use of mobile phones inside classrooms and laboratories is strictly prohibited.

KEY BUILDINGS & INFRASTRUCTURE:
- Centre for Advanced Studies & St. Pauls Block: Houses specialized labs, ICT-enabled classrooms, and seminar halls.
- Main Administrative Block: Handles student affairs, fee payments, ID card issues, and certificate verifications. Open 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Central Library: An expansive resource center. Strict silence must be maintained.
- Canteen / Mess Hall: Provides meals to hostellers and day scholars.
- Hostels: Separate blocks for boys and girls with strict curfew rules.

ACADEMICS & EXAMINATIONS:
- Semester Duration: Each semester consists of approximately 75 working days (or 500 hours).
- Attendance: A minimum of 75% attendance is required to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
- Departments: Includes Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), Electronics and Communication (ECE), Mechanical, and more.

EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS:
- Medical Emergency: A medical dispensary is available on campus. In case of an emergency, students should contact their Head of Department (HOD) or the nearest faculty member immediately.
- Fire/Safety: Assembly points are located in the open grounds near the main blocks.
